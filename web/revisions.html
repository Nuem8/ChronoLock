Revision log

1.1.0 November 12, 2026

A secret can be locked to a public key the recipient already holds. The private key stays in their browser, apart from the public key, and ChronoLock never receives it. A key saved in this browser locks new secrets by default, and you confirm the private key is saved before this browser stores it. A secret can be opened with that key or an unencrypted private-key file. Help opens from the ? in the upper right.

1.0.2 November 12, 2026

The version number is shown beside the title. This log is linked from the bottom of the page.

1.0.0 November 11, 2026

First release. A secret is encrypted in the browser. The key stays in the link. ChronoLock stores the ciphertext until one reveal or the lifetime ends. There is no account and no telemetry.
